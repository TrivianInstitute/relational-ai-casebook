# Discussion questions

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Facilitator notes](facilitator-notes.md)

1. What can be acknowledged without assuming human-equivalent reciprocity?

2. What evidence would help distinguish chosen solitude from a harmful narrowing of options?

3. What obligations arise when a provider markets continuity or connection?

4. How can the friend express concern while preserving Noor's dignity and agency?

5. Would exporting conversations address the loss? Which aspects might it preserve or fail to preserve?

Finish by comparing two defensible courses of action. State which uncertainty is factual, which disagreement concerns values, and which question concerns authority. A good answer can remain conditional.
