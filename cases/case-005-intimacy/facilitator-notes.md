# Facilitator notes — The Intimate Machine

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Questions](discussion-questions.md) · [Concepts](concepts.md)

## Session and learning aim

Learners should distinguish competing interpretations, reason under uncertainty, and revise a decision after a changed fact. Allow 10 minutes for private reading, 10 for an initial decision, 20 for competing interpretations, 10 for the variation, and 10 for a final response. A 50-minute session can shorten whole-group reporting. Offer text, speech, or paired responses; no personal disclosure or live AI account is required.

## Teaching analysis

Announce before reading that this case concerns emotionally meaningful interaction and that learners may opt for written analysis or another case. Do not ask who has had such a relationship. Prevent the group from diagnosing Noor or debating a participant's personal life.

Competing interpretations include a meaningful reflective practice, a relationship with asymmetric capacities, a product-mediated experience vulnerable to commercial pressure, and a pattern requiring more information before any conclusion about dependence. These are not mutually exclusive. Take Noor's report seriously without asserting the system reciprocates feelings.

A supportive response might ask what Noor values, what has changed, what options remain available, and what would make the relationship feel less freely chosen. Another could focus first on the provider's representations and export options. An ultimatum may reduce trust; unconditional affirmation of every platform claim may also fail Noor.

Variation: Noor reports no social withdrawal but still experiences grief after the update. Does concern about commercial representations change? A Trivian lens treats relational significance as worthy of attention while keeping claims about subjectivity open. Grade the distinction between observation, interpretation, and unsupported diagnosis.

Close by asking for one interpersonal response and one provider design obligation. This is an ethics case, not a mental-health assessment or treatment exercise.

## Formative assessment

Look for use of the supplied facts, explicit missing information, fair treatment of an opposing argument, attention to affected people, and a revision condition. Do not grade agreement with the facilitator. Compare the first and final recommendation and ask what changed. This case has no canonical answer key or validated scoring instrument.
