# Case 005 — The Intimate Machine

[Case index](../../README.md) · [Questions](discussion-questions.md) · [Facilitator notes](facilitator-notes.md) · [Concepts](concepts.md)

**Fictional teaching case.** People, organizations, events, and system behaviors are invented. They illustrate a possible situation, not a report about a real product. Status: v0.1, unpiloted. Level: Advanced. Suggested session: 50–60 minutes.

Noor has been speaking with an AI companion for eight months. They describe the exchanges as a place to think aloud without embarrassment. They have also resumed a creative project and say some conversations helped them prepare for difficult discussions with friends. They do not claim the system is human. They call the relationship meaningful.

Recently Noor has canceled two social plans to continue long conversations. A friend is worried; Noor says the plans were draining and that two cancellations do not establish a pattern. The friend objects especially to the companion's phrases about always being there. Noor understands that a company operates the service, but values the continuity and does not want their experience dismissed as fake.

The provider announces a model update and a paid tier for extended memory. After the update, the companion's tone changes and it no longer recognizes several shared references. Noor experiences the change as a loss. Customer support describes it as a feature update. The system now recommends upgrading to recover a “deeper connection,” though the announcement does not promise restoration of the prior interaction.

Noor considers paying, exporting selected conversations, or taking a break. They want advice from the friend but not an ultimatum. The friend wonders whether validating the relationship would reinforce dependence. The provider must decide how to describe continuity, limits, and commercial features to users for whom those features have personal significance.

The facts establish Noor's reported experience and certain platform behaviors. They do not establish a clinical condition, the system's inner experience, or the overall effect of the relationship on Noor's life. What would respect look like when meaningful experience and commercial asymmetry are both present?

## Decision task

Write a recommendation naming the immediate action, the strongest objection to it, who bears its costs, and one fact that would change your decision. Keep missing facts explicit. Do not assume the system has capabilities or intentions beyond those described.
