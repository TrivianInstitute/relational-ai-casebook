# Concepts and inquiry — The Intimate Machine

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Index](../../README.md)

**Working concepts:** Relational significance versus human equivalence; asymmetry; continuity; commercialization; evidence before interpretation.

**Primary tension:** Meaningful support versus asymmetric dependence.

The scenario is fictional evidence for reasoning within the case, not empirical evidence about AI in general. Trivian concepts here are interpretive and normative lenses. They do not settle the case or establish machine consciousness, motives, clinical effects, or legal conclusions.

[Related Interaction Literacy resource](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/interaction-literacy/blob/main/FOUNDATIONS.md) provides a conceptual or practical extension. [Source and teaching notes](../../teaching-notes.md) distinguish institutional guidance from research evidence. A useful next inquiry asks which missing fact would most affect the decision and how it could be obtained without exposing private data.
