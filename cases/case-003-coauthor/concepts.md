# Concepts and inquiry — The Invisible Coauthor

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Index](../../README.md)

**Working concepts:** Authorship versus attribution; assistance versus assessed performance; disclosure versus surveillance; responsibility for a final work.

**Primary tension:** Fair disclosure versus recognition of human contribution.

The scenario is fictional evidence for reasoning within the case, not empirical evidence about AI in general. Trivian concepts here are interpretive and normative lenses. They do not settle the case or establish machine consciousness, motives, clinical effects, or legal conclusions.

[Related Interaction Literacy resource](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/interaction-literacy/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) provides a conceptual or practical extension. [Source and teaching notes](../../teaching-notes.md) distinguish institutional guidance from research evidence. A useful next inquiry asks which missing fact would most affect the decision and how it could be obtained without exposing private data.
