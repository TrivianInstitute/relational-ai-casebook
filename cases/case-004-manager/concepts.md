# Concepts and inquiry — The Manager Who Delegated Judgment

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Index](../../README.md)

**Working concepts:** Nominal versus substantive review; input quality; accountability; procedural fairness; comparative baselines.

**Primary tension:** Consistency and efficiency versus contestable judgment.

The scenario is fictional evidence for reasoning within the case, not empirical evidence about AI in general. Trivian concepts here are interpretive and normative lenses. They do not settle the case or establish machine consciousness, motives, clinical effects, or legal conclusions.

[Related Interaction Literacy resource](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/interaction-literacy/blob/main/curriculum/02-capability-authority-delegation.md) provides a conceptual or practical extension.
