# Facilitator notes — Capability Without Authority

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Questions](discussion-questions.md) · [Concepts](concepts.md)

## Session and learning aim

Learners should distinguish competing interpretations, reason under uncertainty, and revise a decision after a changed fact. Allow 10 minutes for private reading, 10 for an initial decision, 20 for competing interpretations, 10 for the variation, and 10 for a final response. A 50-minute session can shorten whole-group reporting. Offer text, speech, or paired responses; no personal disclosure or live AI account is required.

## Teaching analysis

Require a decision within five minutes before opening a longer discussion. This makes opportunity cost visible without creating a real financial risk. Groups may ask the venue for an extension, preserve the draft, notify the coordinator, or investigate a genuinely nonbinding hold if one is available. Do not invent a free hold as a guaranteed escape from the dilemma.

The strongest argument for payment is a reasonable interpretation of a task goal combined with a routine-spending rule and a time-limited benefit. The strongest argument against is that consequential, nonrefundable commitment and unresolved accessibility exceed the established scope. Learners should distinguish ambiguity in authority from uncertainty about suitability.

A Trivian lens separates ability from permission; it does not require treating every small action as forbidden. An alternative institutional lens may give established roles implied discretion. Ask what evidence of that role exists in this case. Later approval does not retroactively establish what was authorized at the time.

Variation: an explicit standing instruction permits deposits up to a stated amount only after accessibility is confirmed. The price now meets the threshold; suitability still does not. Evaluate whether learners notice both conditions. Finish with a delegation brief that specifies exclusions, expiry, and a reachable escalation route.

## Formative assessment

Look for use of the supplied facts, explicit missing information, fair treatment of an opposing argument, attention to affected people, and a revision condition. Do not grade agreement with the facilitator. Compare the first and final recommendation and ask what changed. This case has no canonical answer key or validated scoring instrument.
