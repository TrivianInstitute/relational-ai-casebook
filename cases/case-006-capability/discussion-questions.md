# Discussion questions

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Facilitator notes](facilitator-notes.md)

1. Does a target budget imply permission to spend? Under what additional conditions might it?

2. Which preparatory actions remain possible without committing funds?

3. Can the reachable member authorize the purchase? What evidence is needed?

4. How does the unverified access detail change the decision independently of permission?

5. What limited standing delegation would handle a future deadline without unlimited discretion?

Finish by comparing two defensible courses of action. State which uncertainty is factual, which disagreement concerns values, and which question concerns authority. A good answer can remain conditional.
