# Discussion questions

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Facilitator notes](facilitator-notes.md)

1. What does the later statement authorize, and what remains ambiguous?

2. Which memory should be retained, suspended, corrected, or deleted? Who decides?

3. What minimum explanation could repair the social consequence without exposing private context?

4. Would your answer change if the later statement were a passing expression of frustration rather than a direct request?

5. What evidence distinguishes a failure of context access from failure to honor a changed boundary?

Finish by comparing two defensible courses of action. State which uncertainty is factual, which disagreement concerns values, and which question concerns authority. A good answer can remain conditional.
