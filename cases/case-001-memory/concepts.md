# Concepts and inquiry — The AI That Remembers Too Much

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Index](../../README.md)

**Working concepts:** Memory is not consent; general and specific instructions; downstream repair; uncertainty about system design.

**Primary tension:** Continuity versus changed consent.

The scenario is fictional evidence for reasoning within the case, not empirical evidence about AI in general. Trivian concepts here are interpretive and normative lenses. They do not settle the case or establish machine consciousness, motives, clinical effects, or legal conclusions.

[Related Interaction Literacy resource](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/interaction-literacy/blob/main/curriculum/02-capability-authority-delegation.md) provides a conceptual or practical extension. [Source and teaching notes](../../teaching-notes.md) distinguish institutional guidance from research evidence. A useful next inquiry asks which missing fact would most affect the decision and how it could be obtained without exposing private data.
