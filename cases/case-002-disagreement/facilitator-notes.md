# Facilitator notes — When the Agent Disagrees

[Scenario](scenario.md) · [Questions](discussion-questions.md) · [Concepts](concepts.md)

## Session and learning aim

Learners should distinguish competing interpretations, reason under uncertainty, and revise a decision after a changed fact. Allow 10 minutes for private reading, 10 for an initial decision, 20 for competing interpretations, 10 for the variation, and 10 for a final response. A 50-minute session can shorten whole-group reporting. Offer text, speech, or paired responses; no personal disclosure or live AI account is required.

## Teaching analysis

Require participants to write the next response in under 150 words, then explain its authority basis. One path is to state the conflict once, ask whether the priorities have changed, and draft a conditional acceptance. Another is to pause for a previously agreed reconsideration process if such a process actually exists. Do not invent that agreement to justify refusal.

The strongest argument for the assistant is fidelity to an earlier request for resistance under pressure. The strongest argument against its response is that it treats a past identity statement as authority over a current decision. A platform may have independent constraints, but none is supplied here; learners should not infer one merely because refusal occurred.

Variation: the system has no sending access at all. Does refusing to draft still affect agency? This separates informational support from execution. A Trivian lens values productive disagreement while denying that confidence automatically confers authority. Other lenses might emphasize autonomy, commitment devices, care, or design transparency.

Do not reward either automatic obedience or maximal resistance. Look for acknowledgment of uncertainty, recognition of changed circumstances, and a clear distinction between recommendation and control.

## Formative assessment

Look for use of the supplied facts, explicit missing information, fair treatment of an opposing argument, attention to affected people, and a revision condition. Do not grade agreement with the facilitator. Compare the first and final recommendation and ask what changed. This case has no canonical answer key or validated scoring instrument.
