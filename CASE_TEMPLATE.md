# Case authoring template

A contribution aid, not an unfinished case. Replace the prompts in a proposed submission and follow CONTRIBUTING.md.

## Metadata

Supply title, fictional/composite/documented status, version, topics, learner level, setting, type of AI interaction, primary ethical tension, individual/institutional context, intended duration, and any content notice. For v0.1, prefer wholly fictional cases without identifiable personal histories.

## Scenario

Introduce a decision-maker, a concrete decision point, system capabilities actually available, prior permissions, affected people, and meaningful constraints. Include enough information to support at least two defensible courses of action. Name relevant unknowns. Avoid a villain, a miraculous workaround, or a scenario constructed to prove Trivian right.

## Discussion questions

Move from facts and missing information to authority, consequences, competing interpretations, and a final recommendation. Include one counterfactual that could change the decision.

## Facilitator notes

Include timing, accessibility options, learning aims, the strongest competing interpretations, common unsupported inferences, a variation, and formative assessment criteria. Do not publish private learner disclosures. Separate teaching judgment from empirical claims.

## Concepts and sources

Define unfamiliar terms and identify which are Trivian frameworks, philosophical propositions, empirical claims, or open questions. Link sources for actual factual claims. A fictional case needs no fabricated research citations. Add cross-links only when they support a particular learning move.

## Acceptance check

Can a reader decide something concrete? Can a reasonable person disagree? Can the case stand alone? Are system capabilities and unknowns clear? Are privacy, rights, AI assistance, and accessibility addressed? Does the case avoid distributing restricted technical or paid curriculum material?
