# Case taxonomy

[Home](README.md)

Choose by topic, level, setting, interaction, tension, or context. Levels indicate discussion complexity, not age eligibility. “Advanced” involves personal or conceptual sensitivity and benefits from experienced facilitation. All six cases are fictional and unpiloted.

| Case | Topics | Learner level | Setting | AI interaction | Primary ethical tension | Context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [001 — The AI That Remembers Too Much](cases/case-001-memory/scenario.md) | memory, continuity, consent, revocation | Introductory | Community organization | Persistent assistant | Continuity versus changed consent | Individual and institutional |
| [002 — When the Agent Disagrees](cases/case-002-disagreement/scenario.md) | agency, refusal, paternalism, authority | Intermediate | Independent creative work | Advisory agent with action access | Protective disagreement versus present agency | Individual |
| [003 — The Invisible Coauthor](cases/case-003-coauthor/scenario.md) | authorship, attribution, disclosure, learning | Intermediate | University | Iterative generative writing | Fair disclosure versus recognition of human contribution | Institutional |
| [004 — The Manager Who Delegated Judgment](cases/case-004-manager/scenario.md) | accountability, delegation, automation bias, power | Intermediate | Workplace | Recommendation system | Consistency and efficiency versus contestable judgment | Institutional |
| [005 — The Intimate Machine](cases/case-005-intimacy/scenario.md) | intimacy, dignity, dependency, continuity | Advanced | Personal life and platform design | Persistent conversational companion | Meaningful support versus asymmetric dependence | Individual and institutional |

Tags are navigation aids, not diagnoses or exhaustive classifications. A future contribution should identify a primary tension and explain why reasonable people could disagree. Add tags only when they help learners choose material.
